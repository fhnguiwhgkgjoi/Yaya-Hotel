Our Concept

Modern Filipino Budget Hotel

Yaya Hotel is a 2 to 3 story building with a minimalist, clean facade thoughtfully designed to stand out in the bustling residential-commercial area of Metro Manila.

Our aesthetic embraces the charm of the modern Filipino lifestyle while prioritizing efficiency. Neutral tones of warm beige and light gray are combined with wood accents and ambient lighting to provide a cozy atmosphere.

With an emphasis on cleanliness, simplicity, and uncompromised comfort, Yaya Hotel is the perfect sanctuary for budget travelers, students, and short-stay guests.
